Kitchen Counter for iOS & Apple Watch

Getting Started

Kitchen Counter scores your pickleball games from your Apple Watch. Open the watch app, set up a game, and use the Digital Crown to score — twist toward you when you score, away when your opponent scores.

Installing the Watch App

The watch app should install automatically when you download Kitchen Counter on your iPhone. If it doesn't appear:

  • Open the Watch app on your iPhone
  • Scroll down to Available Apps
  • Find Kitchen Counter and tap Install

Make sure your Apple Watch is nearby, connected, and has enough storage.

Syncing Between Devices

Game history syncs automatically between your Apple Watch and iPhone via iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed into the same Apple ID with iCloud enabled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work without an Apple Watch?

You can log games manually on your iPhone, but the real-time scoring experience requires an Apple Watch.

Does it track my workouts?

Yes. Kitchen Counter automatically logs each session as a pickleball workout in Apple Health. It counts toward your Activity rings.

Is my data private?

Completely. Your data stays on your devices and in your private iCloud account. We never see it. Read our Privacy Policy.
