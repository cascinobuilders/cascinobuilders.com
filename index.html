StairSmith

iPhone & iPad

A stair calculator for builders, DIYers, and dads with a saw.

Type in your total rise. StairSmith does the math, draws the stairs, checks them against code (IRC, IBC, NBC, Part K or NCC) and gives you a cut sheet to take to the saw.

Kitchen Counter

Apple Watch & iPhone

Pickleball scoring on your Apple Watch.

Twist the Digital Crown toward you when you win the point, away when they do. You’ll feel a tap on your wrist each time it counts. Side-out or rally, doubles or singles. Games save to Apple Health as workouts and sync to your iPhone.
