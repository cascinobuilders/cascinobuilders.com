Privacy Policy

StairSmith for iOS & iPad

Effective: April 27, 2026

TL;DR

StairSmith collects nothing. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your projects live only on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing. StairSmith does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or location data.

What's Stored on Your Device

The stair projects you create and your app preferences (default units, default building code) are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard frameworks:

  • Projects are stored in the app's SwiftData container.
  • Preferences are stored in UserDefaults.

This data never leaves your device. If you delete StairSmith, your projects and preferences are deleted with it.

What We Don't Do

  • No account creation. Ever.
  • No iCloud sync (in v1.0).
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs.
  • No advertising. There are no ads in StairSmith and we do not share data with ad networks.
  • No crash reporting beyond Apple's standard, opt-in, anonymous crash reports — which are managed by your device's iOS settings, not by StairSmith.

Sharing

When you tap "Export PDF" or use the iOS share sheet, your stair calculation is converted to a PDF and handed to iOS for sharing. What happens after that depends on which app or destination you choose (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop, Print, etc.). StairSmith itself never uploads anything.

App Store Reviews

StairSmith may, after your first PDF export and a brief delay, ask whether you'd like to leave an App Store review. This is the standard Apple-provided review prompt; StairSmith does not learn what you choose, what rating you leave, or whether you wrote a review.

Children's Privacy

StairSmith is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone — children or otherwise — because it does not collect any data at all.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional iCloud sync), we'll update this page and the in-app About section, and we'll only collect data with your explicit consent.

Questions

Email us at support@cascinobuilders.com.
