Privacy Policy

Rep Track for iOS · Effective March 1, 2026

The Short Version

Rep Track does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device. That's it.

Data Collection

Rep Track does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to any external servers.

Data Storage

Your workout data — exercises, sets, reps, and weights — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device. If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it.

Third-Party Services

Rep Track does not integrate with any third-party services. The app uses Apple's built-in StoreKit API solely to occasionally prompt you for an App Store review. This is a standard Apple system feature that does not collect or transmit any personal data.

Children's Privacy

Since Rep Track does not collect data from any user, it does not collect data from children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Given the app's design, significant changes are unlikely.
